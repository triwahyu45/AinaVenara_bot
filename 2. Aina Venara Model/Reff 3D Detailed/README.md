# Aina Venara Detailed 3D Reference Pack

Paket ini adalah panduan visual modeling Aina Venara versi kanon untuk workflow
VRoid, Blender, dan VRM. Sheet dibuat sebagai reference modeling stylized, bukan
blueprint CAD dengan ukuran absolut.

## Canonical Design Lock

- Short layered cyan bob hair with blue-violet lower tips.
- Teal-green eyes, subtle blush, and thin rounded pink glasses.
- Metallic number-3 hairclip on the front-left hair side.
- Zigzag ahoge.
- Open oversized cyan-to-mint hoodie with charcoal off-shoulder collar and cuffs.
- White inner shirt, dark shorts, white crew socks, and white low-top sneakers.

## Files

- `01_Aina_FullBody_Orthographic.png`: primary full-body turnaround.
- `02_Aina_Head_Face_Detail.png`: face, glasses, hairline, and head close-ups.
- `03_Aina_Outfit_Construction.png`: clothing layers and outfit construction.
- `04_Aina_Accessories_Materials.png`: accessory geometry and material guidance.
- `05_Aina_Silhouette_Proportions.png`: silhouette and conceptual 152 cm ratio guide.
- `06_Aina_Hair_Construction.png`: hair layer breakdown and strand direction guide.
- `07_Aina_Body_Dimension_Spec.png`: conceptual body measurements and rig baseline.
- `08_Aina_Head_Face_Measurements.png`: face, glasses, and hairclip measurement targets.
- `09_Aina_Hair_Garment_Dimensions.png`: hair, hoodie, garment, and footwear dimensions.
- `10_Aina_Hands_Feet_Micro_Detail.png`: hand topology, socks, and sneaker close-ups.
- `Aina_Venara_3D_Reference_Index.png`: quick visual overview.
- `Aina_Venara_3D_Reference_Index_Extended.png`: overview of all ten visual sheets.
- `Aina_Venara_Modeling_Spec.md`: complete written metric specification.

## Recommended Modeling Order

1. Start from `01_Aina_FullBody_Orthographic.png`.
2. Lock body scale and silhouette with `05_Aina_Silhouette_Proportions.png`.
3. Shape face and glasses with `02_Aina_Head_Face_Detail.png`.
4. Build the hair layers with `06_Aina_Hair_Construction.png`.
5. Build clothing layers with `03_Aina_Outfit_Construction.png`.
6. Polish accessories and materials with `04_Aina_Accessories_Materials.png`.
7. Apply the measurement targets from sheets `07` through `10`.

## Notes

The old turnaround is intentionally preserved in the sibling `Reff 3D` folder.
Generated visual plates are retained under `_source/generated` for traceability.
Titles, palette swatches, and modeling notes were composed locally to keep the
technical labels deterministic.

All numeric values are conceptual targets for a fictional stylized character.
They are intended to keep Blender and VRoid iterations consistent, not to claim
measured real-person anatomy.
