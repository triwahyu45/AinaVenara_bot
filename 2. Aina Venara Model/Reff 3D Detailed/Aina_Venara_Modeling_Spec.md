# Aina Venara Canonical Modeling Specification

Status: conceptual target specification for VRoid, Blender, and VRM construction.
These are design-lock values for a fictional stylized character, not measured
real-person anatomy. Small deviations are acceptable when needed for deformation
quality, but the silhouette and signature details should remain stable.

## Modeling Priority

1. Lock the 152 cm scale and relaxed A-pose.
2. Match the petite silhouette before adding garment volume.
3. Preserve the bob silhouette, lower-tip gradient, ahoge, and number-3 clip.
4. Build the hoodie as an oversized garment around the slim torso.
5. Keep glasses, collar, cuffs, socks, and sneakers readable but visually quiet.
6. Test deformation before finalizing weights and spring bones.
